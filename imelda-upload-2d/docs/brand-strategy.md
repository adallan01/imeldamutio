# Brand strategy: Imelda Mutio

## Positioning

**For** organisers of corporate events, conferences, panels, galas, brand activations and community sessions,
**Imelda Mutio** is the Nairobi radio presenter and MC who brings **the warmth and quick thinking of live radio** to the room,
**so that** the audience stays connected and the programme keeps moving.

**Line:** Good conversations. Great audiences. (from her own portfolio)
**Sign-off:** Good conversations. Great audiences. Real impact.

## Pillars (from her portfolio)

| Pillar | Meaning for a client | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Relatable | Connects with very different audiences | Daily listener conversation on radio |
| Engaging | Keeps people informed, entertained and involved | Interviews and the Dilemma of the Day |
| Versatile | Studio, corporate session, workshop or festival stage | Event photos across formats |
| Professional | Reliable, prepared, adaptable | Five hours of live radio each weekday |

## Design

The site deliberately reuses Brian Aseli's design system so the two can be managed the same way: stage navy, beam blue and copper; Sora, Manrope and Instrument Serif; a Tecno-style cinematic hero (three slides here); stage-light section backgrounds that fade into each other; photos shown in brand blue with true colour on hover; light and dark mode; first-person copy with no em dashes.

Differences from Brian's site:
- Three hero slides (green shirt, orange blazer, trench coat cut-outs).
- No video carousels yet (her clips are station-owned, on Facebook). An **On air** page and homepage section tell the radio story instead, with a link to her Instagram clips.
- **Gallery** replaces Watch; **On air** replaces Credits and Press, because no event credits are confirmed yet.

## Independence

Same safeguards as Brian's site: no station logo or artwork, career-move news kept off, the broadcast service marked "subject to employer policy", and the footer disclaimer.
